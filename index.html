Before work begins

Gather the paperwork before you ask for work.

Wiring changes do not always leave a record. In a recently purchased home, the previous owner may have added circuits, moved fixtures or finished a basement without drawings, and labels someone else wrote can describe an old layout, not what each breaker controls.

Whatever kind of home you have, gather any home inspection report, ESA notification paperwork and invoices from earlier electrical work. Keep them in one folder with a photo of the panel labels, where the next owner or contractor can find them.

Which electrical records should I ask the seller for?

Ask for drawings, inspection reports, invoices and any ESA notification paperwork for earlier work. If the seller has none, say so in your enquiry, because a contractor can then plan extra time to assess what is already installed.

Can I rely on breaker labels left by a previous owner?

Treat them as a lead, not a fact. A label may describe a layout that was later changed, so ask the contractor to confirm each circuit during the assessment and to record the result in writing.

What should two written scopes both name before I choose?

Put the written scopes side by side and check that each names the same circuits, equipment and notification step. A quote that leaves out a record or label update is not the same as one that includes it.

Who needs to approve work if the home was just purchased?

The registered owner normally authorizes the work, but a lender, an insurer or a condominium corporation may also need to be told. Name who can approve changes and who can open the door on the visit.

After I send the form, whose name goes on the work agreement?

Any agreement names a contractor, never this site. Treat the site as a guidebook that reviews enquiries but neither arranges visits nor sets prices, and performs no electrical work. Confirm separately with any provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor its identity and licence; check the licence through ESA before agreeing. Settle scope and cost with it directly, dates included.
